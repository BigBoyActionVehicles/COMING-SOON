About Big Boy

Who we are, and what we bring to your production.

Big Boy Action Vehicles supplies vehicles to the entertainment and photographic industries — from single hero cars for a print campaign to full convoys for a feature film chase sequence. We work with production companies, agencies, photographers and studios who need a vehicle that looks right, performs on cue, and turns up exactly when the call sheet says it will. Our vehicle spans muscle cars, monster trucks, stunt-modified rigs and everyday vehicles dressed for the shot, and every one of them is maintained, insured and permitted for on-set and location work before it ever reaches you. We handle the logistics that productions don't have time for: transport, on-set drivers where needed, camera-mount preparation, and the paperwork that keeps health and safety off your list of worries. Big Boy started because we kept seeing productions lose days chasing down the right vehicle from three different suppliers — so we built a vehicle and a team that could be the one call you need to make instead.
