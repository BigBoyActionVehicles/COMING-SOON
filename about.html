About Big Boy Action Vehicles

We Know the Industry. We Know What Productions Needs.

Big Boy Action Vehicles was built from decades of experience on set. With over 30 years working across film and television, Danny brings a rare understanding of what productions actually need from their vehicles. His experience spans vehicle coordination, acting, special effects, art department and writing, giving Big Boy a unique perspective on how vehicles need to look, perform and work within a production. From a single hero vehicle for a commercial or photo shoot to a fleet of vehicles for a feature film, we take care of the details that can make or break a shoot. We source the right vehicle, prepare it for camera, coordinate transport and towing, and can provide experienced on-set vehicle wranglers, drivers and mechanics to keep everything running smoothly. We also specialise in custom picture-vehicle builds, creating everything from period and character vehicles to heavily modified and completely bespoke machines designed specifically for the screen. Our goal is simple: make the vehicle department one less thing production has to worry about. With Big Boy, you’re not just hiring a vehicle — you’re getting a team that understands production.
